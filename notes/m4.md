## 01/11 afternoon — frame-by-frame review of the demo recording, bug fixes, smoothness check

Recorded the demo (current JS embedded into the Release build, `tools/perf/embed.sh`, sim "Polish 17 Pro Max" since Neon B was deleted),
extracted the original frames (not through the `fps` filter — on VFR video it duplicates frames, which looks like "skipping") and
reviewed each segment.

**Bugs fixed** (`src/scene/NeonScene.tsx`, `src/neon/Tube.tsx`, `src/neon/atlas.ts`):
1. **Scene time read from JS lags.** `time.get()` on the JS thread is an asynchronously updated copy; under load it lags ~0.3 s of scene
   time → the new heart tube appears already 90 % grown (losing the "freeze" effect), and the same bug can make the recolour gas start
   midway and swallow the first flash on power-on. Now: events from worklets (demo, hand-drawn strokes) pass the frame's `t` directly;
   events from JS (switch tap, colour tap) read time via `runOnUISync` (`nowUI`). Applied to `commit` (born), `recolor` (flowAt),
   `toggle` (powerAt/offAt).
2. **Empty frames on finger lift.** The chalk trail + finger dot vanished instantly before the tube appeared → 3–5 empty frames. Now the
   chalk trail fades over 0.45 s (`TRAIL_FADE`) exactly as the tube grows along the stroke, the virtual finger fades over 0.28 s;
   hand-drawn strokes keep `pts` until the next stroke.
3. **Palette covering the heart.** The palette was placed 58 pt from the touch point, so it landed between the two heart lobes. Now it is
   placed by the top edge of the whole tube (14 pt away), avoiding other tubes' bounding boxes; if there's no room it goes below the tube,
   and only as a last resort to the old position.
4. **Double-bright dot at tube segment joins** (heart tip, where start and end of a closed tube meet; clearly visible in the puddle
   reflection too). Each segment's baked glow and the lighting atlas used round stroke caps and then added (plus) → two half-circles
   overlapping. Now butt caps: the two blurred halves add up seamlessly. Gas/glass body still uses round caps.

**Reviewed, not bugs:** red glow around the "o" while the letters ignite = far light from the heart (hello uses units 0–4, heart units
5–6, no overlap); pink/white "o" at the end during recolour = the cyan gas front arriving.

**Smoothness:** the measured build (PERF overlay) 58 fps, longest frame 16.7 ms, 0 dropped frames through the whole demo even at load
5–6. The "stalls" in the real-time recording come from the recorder / SimRenderServer competing for the GPU with other running simulators,
not the app. Recording at `EXPO_PUBLIC_TIMESCALE=0.5` then compressing to 60 fps (the `tools/rec.sh` approach): **60/60 distinct frames
per second from start to end**, even under load.
